This game hasn't even been released yet. Nobody has played it. Not even for a single minute. And yet, it has reportedly already generated hundreds of millions of dollars in anticipation. In just 12 days, after thirteen long years of waiting, GTA 6 is finally arriving. A gigantic map, insane records, a controversial price… and even one detail that could force some players to delete half the games on their consoles. So here are 10 things you absolutely need to know before November 19. And trust me, the last one could seriously hurt.

First, 13 years of waiting. GTA 5 was released in September 2013. Ever since, players have been waiting for a proper sequel. Originally scheduled for fall 2025, GTA 6 was first pushed back to May 26, 2026, before being delayed again. The current release date is Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X and S. Thirteen years of waiting. And this time, Rockstar says the date is set.

Second, trailers that are smashing every record. December 2023. The first trailer leaks online just before its official release. The result? More than 90 million views in just 24 hours. A Guinness World Record for a video game trailer. But the second trailer goes even further, reaching 475 million views in a single day across all platforms. To put that into perspective, that's more than the views recorded by the Deadpool and Wolverine trailer. And the game isn't even out yet!

Third, welcome to Leonida. This time, we're heading to Leonida, a fictional state inspired by Florida. At the heart of it all is Vice City, a reimagined version of the Miami fans already know. But it's not just going to be skyscrapers and beautiful beaches. Swamps, alligators, residential neighborhoods, and completely different environments… Rockstar wants to recreate an entire state, not just a city.

Fourth, a map that could be absolutely massive. According to estimates circulating online, the map could be roughly twice the size of GTA 5's. And that's not all. Some comparisons even suggest a massive scale relative to Red Dead Redemption 2. Vice City alone is expected to be enormous. Imagine the possibilities: hours of exploration, hidden locations, and activities everywhere. But a huge map means nothing without a great story…

Fifth, for the first time, two main characters in a couple. No more switching between three protagonists like in GTA 5. This time, we follow Jason and Lucia, a couple caught up in a criminal story. Their relationship is at the heart of the adventure. And Rockstar seems to be bringing back some elements from Red Dead Redemption 2, including a moral dimension to the player's choices. In other words, your decisions could have far more consequences than before.

Sixth, how much will it cost? Let's talk money. The Standard Edition is listed at $79.99, while the Ultimate Edition reaches $99.99. But here's something you should know: some announced physical editions may not include a disc, just a download code. Yes, you could pay for a physical box without actually getting the game on a disc.

Seventh, GTA 6 could already have millions of pre-orders. According to figures attributed to Sensor Tower, the game has reportedly reached around 7.7 million pre-orders. The benchmark record still belongs to Cyberpunk 2077, with approximately 8 million pre-orders. That means GTA 6 would need only around 300,000 more copies to reach that figure. And the craziest part is…

Eighth, PC players aren't even part of the equation. Yes, you heard that right. No PC version has been officially announced for launch. The game is targeting PS5 and Xbox Series consoles first. Just think about it: millions of reported pre-orders, records being discussed… while a massive portion of the gaming community is still waiting for its turn.

Ninth, Rockstar is everywhere, even without advertising. The hype surrounding GTA 6 has become so massive that brands are trying to ride the wave. Between Vice City references, marketing campaigns, and unexpected Easter eggs, even competitors want a piece of the attention. Xbox reportedly took things so far that it featured a console inside an alligator. Yes, an alligator! But wait, we're getting to the part that directly affects your console.

Tenth, make room on your storage drive. According to estimates circulating online, GTA 6 could require up to 212 GB of storage space. And if you thought you could simply wait until release day to install it, the pre-load is reportedly expected to begin on November 10. So if your storage is already full, you know what you need to do. Because the worst thing would be finding out you don't have enough space on the very day everyone starts playing.

BONUS: What about the third trailer? So far, there's still no official third trailer. Meanwhile, some projections suggest the game could sell up to 50 million copies in its first week. If those estimates become reality, GTA 6 could deliver one of the biggest launches in entertainment history. Thirteen years of waiting for a game that could rewrite the record books.
